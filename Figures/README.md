Figures created during the development of the paper will be separated on two folders, one for the ones presented on the paper proper, and one with unused figures, which will be considered the Supplementary Material:

- Paper/
  1. [Figure 1](https://github.com/RaphaLevy/Environmental_Drivers_of_Malaria/blob/main/Figures/Paper/total_active_infectious_cases_manaus_2017_2023.png): Total number of active infectious cases of malaria (_P. vivax_, SIVEP-Malaria) in rural Manaus, 2017-2023. The strong inter- and 
intra-annual variability of the disease burden is the central modelling challenge addressed in this work, and motivates the representation of all 
of the variability of the environmental drivers as well as the modifications to the baseline model.
  2. [Figure 2](https://github.com/RaphaLevy/Environmental_Drivers_of_Malaria/blob/main/Figures/Paper/weekly_beta_calibration_2017.png): Comparison between the observed active cases and the model estimation of the infectious human compartment $I_H$ for 2017.
  3. [Figure 3](https://github.com/RaphaLevy/Environmental_Drivers_of_Malaria/blob/main/Figures/Paper/coupled_and_human_Rt_variation_in_2017_with_months.png): Seasonal variation of the coupled and human basic reproduction number
$\mathcal{R}(t)$ for the calibrated model in 2017.
  4. [Figure 4](https://github.com/RaphaLevy/Environmental_Drivers_of_Malaria/blob/main/Figures/Paper/local_sensitivity_R0_IH.png): Normalized sensitivity index $S_x = \partial\log Y/\partial\log x$ of the 25 decomposed parameters (bars coloured by parameter group, as in Table 3), where $Y$ is the output being differentiated: $Y = \mathcal{R}\_{0}$ for the closed-form reproduction number in the left panel and $Y = Q\_{\text{peak}}$ for the seasonal maximum of the infectious-human compartment of the calibrated model in the right panel. Left: closed-form reproduction number at the reference environmental state, by central differences with $h = 0.05$; the exact analytic values of Table 3 differ from these by at most $0.06$, the truncation error of the difference quotient. Right: seasonal maximum of the infectious-human compartment of the calibrated model, averaged over 2017-2023, same scheme. A positive index means the output increases with the parameter. The three _Anopheles_ suitability temperatures are exactly zero on the left because the thermal-suitability factor $f\_T$ is saturated at the reference climate.
